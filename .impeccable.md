# Design context — Lord of Gyms

Derived from `docs/PRD.md` and decisions the owner made in session. Correct anything wrong;
the design skills read this file.

## Target audience

Two audiences, and they are not the same people.

**The console** is used by gym staff in Nepal — front desk (highest volume, all day, often
on a cheap laptop with a member waiting at the counter), branch managers, trainers, and the
chain owner who checks reports a few times a week. Chains of 3-50 branches, 500-20,000
members. Sales-led, not self-serve. Not tech-sophisticated; speed and legibility beat
elegance.

**The printed documents** are for the member — a person handed a piece of A4 across the
counter after paying, often in cash. They may keep it for months, show it to a spouse who
paid, or submit it for corporate reimbursement. Many read English as a second language.
They are not the software's user and will never see the console.

## Use cases

- Register a walk-in and sell them a plan in under 90 seconds, while they stand there.
- Take a cash payment and hand over paper that proves it.
- Show a member what they owe, and what they saved, without them having to ask.
- Let an owner see collection and arrears across branches without phoning managers.

## Brand personality / tone

Plain, exact, and quietly confident. This product replaces a register book and an Excel
sheet — it earns trust by being unambiguous, not by being clever. No marketing voice in the
product. Money is stated, never softened.

**The printed sheet specifically:** it is a financial record first. Restraint, not
decoration. Strong horizontal structure, real typographic hierarchy, tabular numerals, one
heavy accent used exactly once (the amount owed). A member glancing at it should read, in
this order: what they bought, what they saved, what they still owe. Nothing on the page
competes with those three.

## Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind v4, Base UI (shadcn-derived). Figtree is the type family.
- Printed documents render through the browser's own print engine. No PDF library. That
  means: no portal-backed UI on the sheet, backgrounds may be dropped unless opted in via
  `print-color-adjust`, and the page is A4 at a 12/14mm margin.
- Documents are deliberately outside the theme system — an invoice must look identical
  whatever the console theme is.
- Money is integer paisa, formatted only at the render boundary. Currency is org-configurable
  (NPR today).
- Nepali (Devanagari) is a planned follow-up. Figtree carries no Devanagari.

## Differentiation

Gym software in this market prints either nothing at all or a thermal-printer slip. A
member walking out with a document that is genuinely well set — and that tells them plainly
what they saved and what they owe — is the thing they remember about the gym's
professionalism.
